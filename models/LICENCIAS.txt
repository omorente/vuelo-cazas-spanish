(hangar FGMEMBERS, https://github.com/FGMEMBERS), convertidos de AC3D a glTF con herramientas/ac2gltf.py y con
libreas retocadas. Se redistribuyen bajo la misma licencia GPL-2.

g103.json   Grob G 103 Twin Astir  https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/G103
cl415.json  Bombardier/Canadair 415 https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/Bombardier-415 (librea original de Gary Brown; modelo de Emmanuel Baranger)
a400.json   Airbus A400M           https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/A400M
a321.json   Airbus A321            https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/A321
rallye.json Morane-Saulnier Rallye MS.893 https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/Rallye-MS893 (sin interior)

Texto de la licencia: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/gpl-2.0.html

ef_ck.json  Cabina 3D del EF2000 (cockpit, paneles, pantallas, HUP, palanca) https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/EF2000 (GPL-2), juntada con herramientas/ensamblar_ac.py
pc21_ck.json Cabina 3D delantera del PC-21 (cabina, instrumentos, CHDD, iSFD, palanca, gases, pedales, tren) https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/PC-21 (GPL-3)
c101_ck.json Interior de la cabina del C-101 (cubeta, panel, parasol, suelo, vigas) https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/C-101 (GPL-3), con el panel de relojes del juego encima
